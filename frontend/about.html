About the project

A closer look at
the model behind it.

Leaf Diagnostics classifies tomato leaf photos into six conditions using a convolutional neural network trained from scratch in PyTorch. Here's how it's built, end to end.

How a scan works

Three steps happen between choosing a photo and seeing a diagnosis.

1

Upload

A leaf photo is selected or dropped in from your device, and previewed before anything is sent.

2

Inference

The image is posted to a Flask/PyTorch backend on Render, where the trained CNN scores it against six classes.

3

Reading

Scores come back, get sorted by confidence, and are rendered as the readout you see on the diagnose page.

The model

A custom CNN — not a fine-tuned off-the-shelf network — built and trained specifically for this dataset in PyTorch, using Google Colab for GPU training.
